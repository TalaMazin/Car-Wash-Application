# Car-Wash-Application
A Flutter-based mobile application designed to simplify car wash service booking and management. The application provides a complete digital platform that connects customers, employees, and managers through a multi-role system.

The app allows customers to easily book car wash services, manage their vehicles, choose convenient service locations, and track their requests in real time. Employees can manage assigned jobs and update service progress, while managers can monitor operations, manage users, and oversee service activities.

## Key Features

- **Multi-Role Authentication**
  - Customer, Employee, and Manager roles with different permissions and experiences.

- **Customer Features**
  - Create and manage personal profiles.
  - Add and manage multiple vehicles.
  - Browse available car wash services.
  - Book appointments based on location and availability.
  - Track ongoing service status in real time.
  - View booking history and previous services.
  - Earn and redeem reward points.

- **Employee Features**
  - View assigned car wash tasks.
  - Update job status and service progress.
  - Manage customer service requests.
  - Track completed and pending jobs.

- **Manager Features**
  - Manage employees and customers.
  - Monitor bookings and service operations.
  - Manage available services and business activities.
  - View system statistics and reports.

- **Location-Based Services**
  - Support for selecting service locations.
  - Enables efficient service delivery based on customer location.

- **Multiple Payment Methods**
  - Cash payment.
  - Visa/Card payments.
  - Digital wallet payments.

- **Reward Points System**
  - Customers earn points through completed bookings.
  - Points can be used for future benefits and discounts.

- **Real-Time Service Tracking**
  - Follow the progress of car wash requests.
  - Receive updates about booking status.

- **Booking Management**
  - Create, update, and review bookings.
  - Maintain complete service history.

## Technologies Used

- Flutter
- Dart
- Firebase
- Git & GitHub

## Project Goal

The goal of this project is to provide a convenient, reliable, and user-friendly solution for managing car wash services digitally while improving communication between customers, employees, and service managers.
